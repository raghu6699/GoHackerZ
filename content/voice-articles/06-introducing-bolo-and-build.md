On Saturday 28 November 2026, RepoRally is hosting Bolo and Build, a one-day hackathon with an unusual rule. You build your app by voice.

Teams will use Wispr Flow to dictate their prompts to a coding agent, ship a working app for one of five problems, and then publish their build log on RepoRally so the next builder can learn from it. "Bolo" means "speak" in Hindi.

The event runs from 10:00 to 18:00 IST, in person in Hyderabad and online for everyone else. Registration is free.

## The idea

Coding agents have changed what a day of building looks like. Much of your time now goes into describing what you want, and speaking is a natural way to do that. This event is a chance to find out how far you can get when you commit to it, and to write down what you learn.

## The five tracks

- **Health:** help people understand a prescription, describe symptoms or find care in their own language.
- **Farmers:** prices, weather and crop advice for people working in the field.
- **Learning:** tools for students and teachers in smaller towns.
- **Small business:** billing, stock and tax records for a shopkeeper with no time to spare.
- **Citizens:** find government schemes, fill forms and file complaints.

For ideas on what to consider in each, read our guide on [voice-first software for Bharat](/article/voice-first-software-for-bharat).

## How it works

- Teams of one to four. Developers, designers and first-time builders are all welcome.
- Use any coding agent and any model. Dictate your prompts with Wispr Flow. Typing is fine for small fixes, but the build should be driven by voice.
- Your app does not need voice features. The voice rule is about how you build it.
- Start from a blank repository. Your repo must be public.
- Submit a repo link, a demo video of three minutes or less, and the link to your build log.
- Publish your build log on RepoRally. It counts toward your score and is required for prizes.

## How you are judged

Judges look at impact and usefulness, a working demo, the quality of your build log and the quality of the design. We are lining up judges from global companies, who will mostly join remotely, and will announce them on the event page.

Awards include first, second and third place, one winner for each track, and honorable mentions for best design and best build log. Everyone who finishes gets a RepoRally Builder Passport and certificate.

## Get ready

- Download and test Wispr Flow before the day, and check your plan limits.
- Bring a headset with a microphone. Many people will be dictating at once.
- Read [how to build software by voice](/article/build-software-by-voice-wispr-flow-guide) and [how to prompt a coding agent by voice](/article/prompting-a-coding-agent-by-voice).
